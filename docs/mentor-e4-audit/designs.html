一表人才 · 学生管理 · same sidebar and colours, denser and faster to work with

A list you can work through

  • List view by default, one row per student, about 40px tall, so a whole advisor group fits on screen. 「卡片」 at the right of the filter bar switches back to cards (bottom screenshot).
  • Advisor groups stay, read from the 备注名 parentheses. Each group header sums it up: 「4 人 · 新生 2 · 新生昨天没填 1 · 3 天以上没填 1」. 「新生」 means signed up within the last 30 days, the students to check every day.
  • Columns line up across groups: 学生, 学校, 阶段 (red 「新生 · 第 N 天」 in the first 30 days, 「每周」 after), 近 7 天 (seven squares, today dashed until it's over, plus N/7), 需关注 (「昨天填了」 / 「昨天没填」 for 新生, 「N 天没填」 from 3 days for the rest), 最后记录 (date and 「3 天前」), 待补填, 连接.
  • Inside each group: connected students first, 新生 at the top of those, then most days without a record.
  • Actions appear on hover (断开, 改备注, ···) instead of sitting on every row. Clicking a row or a name opens the side panel.
  • One filter bar: search, 学校, 连接, advisor chips and the view switch in a single row.
  • Keyboard: J/K move between students, Enter opens one, / searches, T opens 今日反馈. In 今日反馈, C copies and E marks as sent. The hints sit at the bottom of the page.
  • 今日反馈 folds into a strip of counts (top screenshot). Opened, it has tabs for 待发, 新生昨天没填, 3 天以上没填 and 已发, with the selected draft highlighted (middle screenshot).

Kept: sidebar, red highlight, glass panels, black main button, status dots, advisor grouping, the side panel. No page header.

Now: cards only, page header, no fill status.

AI 草稿 · tuned against the real model

Drafts that sound like a mentor typing on WeChat

The prompt was run against your DashScope model (qwen3.8-flash, from .env.local) on six sample students over five rounds, 66 drafts in all, until the drafts stopped sounding written. The samples include notes in a student's own words, a record with no notes, practices still missing 实测, and a week with nothing to complain about. No real student data was used.

  • Written as the mentor talking: short, plain, 「你」, no name, no 「同学」.
  • One concrete thing from the records, using the student's own words from their notes. Actions the notes don't mention (改对, 标出来, 重做) can't be claimed.
  • No stock praise or report language: 加油, 继续保持, 值得表扬, 展现, 体现, 态度, 习惯, 说明, 目标 and similar are blocked. Drafts that still contain them, or run long, are rewritten once automatically.
  • Daily: praise only, no advice and no questions. Weekly: what went well, then at most one question, and only about a problem the code found (自评 above 实测, 实测 not filled, a subject untouched, days without records). If the code finds none, the weekly draft is praise only.

The side panel shows everything the student filled in

  • The three tiles show 近 7 天有记录, 待补填 and 最后记录 instead of all-time totals.
  • While the panel is open, J/K step to the next or previous student in the list.
  • Records are grouped by date. Each row shows what the student entered: start and end time, course, 学/复/练, 形式, chapter and unit, 备注, duration, 主观, and 客观 (or 待补填 / 不适用).
